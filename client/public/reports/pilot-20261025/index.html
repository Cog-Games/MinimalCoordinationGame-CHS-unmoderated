3. Practice and comprehension

Four retained paired-practice trials followed by 16 main trials. Failed practice attempts are separate and included in replay; they are not added to these 20 columns.

Numbers show answer attempts; all completers eventually answered correctly. M011 needed six attempts on big-kitchen cooperation, M012 two; M013 needed four on small-kitchen cake quantity. Eventual correction does not establish first-attempt understanding, and rapid repeat clicks are not independent belief judgments.

All three new children experienced successful cooperation in practice. Their subsequent choices therefore cannot be explained simply by never having experienced cooperation. Trust, risk preference, comprehension and path costs remain possible factors; this small observational sample and nonrandom version changes cannot identify mechanisms or the causal effect of revised practice.

4.1 Clear-to-Hare cases

There are seven new CH paths: four from M012 and three from M013, for eight cumulatively including M008. CH means clear-branch departure followed by a Hare endpoint. Attributing a switch to observed AI defection requires inspecting AI actions visible before the switch; the final dyad outcome alone is insufficient. Replay includes all paths, and the trial table lists categories and immediate reversals.
