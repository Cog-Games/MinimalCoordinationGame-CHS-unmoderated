1. Final dyad outcomes

Outcomes are ordered child/AI. The four-child mean weights each child's 16 trials equally. Children cooperated successfully on 30/64 trials (46.9%); parameter-matched self-play achieved 67.3% (16,000 trials). SH/HS annotations describe endpoint combinations, not evidence of intentional defection.

1.1 Total cakes earned by each dyad

Using SS=5, HH=2, and SH/HS=1, the total is 200 cakes, averaging 50 per dyad. In the Excel export, cooperative trials have totalReward=10 because each player is assigned 5 points; this does not mean 10 shared cakes.

2.5 New finding: cooperation experience does not eliminate persistent small-kitchen choices

All four completers experienced successful cooperation. M008 even had an additional successful cooperative attempt in practice round 3, yet chose the small kitchen on all 16 main-task trials. Thus, never experiencing successful cooperation cannot fully explain M008's behavior. The effects of practice failures, risk preferences, and partner expectations remain unresolved.

4. M008: the only Clear → Hare case

4.1 M02 · Main-task trial 11: the AI continues toward Stag while the child turns back

The child moved from [8,0] along the clear branch to [4,1], then returned to [4,0] on joint turn 6. Before that turn, the AI was at [4,7]; it then moved to [4,6]. The AI reached the big kitchen at [4,4] on turn 8 and stayed there. The child reached the small kitchen at [6,3] on turn 17.

This is not a case of the child withdrawing after the AI chose the small kitchen:The AI did not go to the small kitchen. However, actions within a turn are simultaneous: the AI's new position in that turn cannot be treated as information already available to the child when choosing that move. The trajectory alone cannot establish the reason for rerouting.

4.2 Interpreting the practice history

M008 answered all comprehension items correctly on the first attempt and cooperated successfully in practice round 2 and the first attempt at round 3. In the main task, the AI chose the big kitchen while the child chose the small kitchen on 7 trials. M008 identified the partner as a computer, rated it a “Bad collaborator,” and did not want to play again. These responses and paths motivate follow-up questions, but do not independently establish distrust or risk aversion.

6.1 Comparability with the previous cohort and baseline

Compared with the previous cohort:Cooperation increased from 12/64 (18.8%) to 30/64 (46.9%); the number of children with at least one successful cooperative trial increased from 1/4 to 3/4. However, participants, practice, and AI parameters all changed: previously cost=0.9 and τ=0.2; now cost=0.3, τ=0.1, and jointStagReward=5. The difference cannot be attributed to practice alone. Figures use saved validation results for the updated Joint-planning self-play baseline, without mixing in old DP/JEV results.

The recorded build commit is 4820e5b. All six files share the same sourceHash and have dirty=true. This analysis uses the supplied Excel snapshots; it does not verify live Google Drive synchronization. The sample is small and results are descriptive. No causal conclusion has been established about individual AI moves causing children's route changes.

6.2 Joint-planning agent comparison

Self-play baseline:1,000 sessions × 16 maps = 16,000 trials, seed=70419327; cost=0.30, τ=0.10, γ=0.9, horizon=60, jointStagReward=5, matching this cohort's main-task decision logs. Cooperation: 10,766/16,000=67.29%; both Hare: 32.71%; mixed endpoints: 0. Mean shared cakes per 16 trials: 64.298. Source: shared-cakes-step-beta3-validation.json and the corresponding release validation notes. Existing production-code validation results were reused; simulations were not rerun for this report. Children played human–AI; the baseline is agent–agent. It describes self-play under matching parameters and cannot independently establish children's ability or the AI's cooperativeness toward children. Path plots use self-play Player 1; dyad plots use both endpoints. Sample sizes and experimental units differ, so no significance test treating trials as independent is performed.
